ASKThree minutes, on your phone

Ten backs of books. Which ones would you read?

No titles. No authors. No covers. Just the paragraph you would get if you picked the book up and turned it over.

Tap the ones that make you want to read the book. Not the ones you think are well written — the ones you would actually carry to the till. Tap nothing if nothing does that: that is a real answer too.

Then hit the red button at the bottom and send me the numbers.

02Why I am asking

I have built a machine that scores blurbs, and it is broken. Every verdict I hold from you is a no, and you cannot calibrate a scorer on nothing but no's. One yes — from any book, mine or somebody else's — is worth more than ten more drafts from me.

Some of these are from books that sold hundreds of thousands of copies. Some are mine. I am not telling you which until you answer, because if I did, the answer would be worth nothing.
