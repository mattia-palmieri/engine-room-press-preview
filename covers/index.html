The shelf — sixteen covers

Every book in the catalogue has a cover. Nothing is written yet, so every one says coming soon — that is the point: the shelf is what we sell before a single book exists.

Four rows, four parts. The field colour tells you the part: Tools and Stories on ink, Communication and Power on blue. Squint at this page on your phone — at thumbnail size you should still read the title and see one imprint, not sixteen books.
