2026-09-01 — Matt's course correction

What he said

One long dictated message while looking at /covers/ on his phone. Five distinct orders inside it:

  1. The ballot framing is wrong. Never tell a reader "we write in the order you choose." It should read: coming soon → want to know when it's ready → email. And explicitly: save the email somewhere simple, the email/database machinery comes later. Focus now = product, design, distribution.
  2. Covers carry too much text. He counted the labels himself: Engine Room Press, 1 of 16, Part 1 Tools, 01, The Essential 16, Coming Soon. All small, all competing, only the title readable.
  3. Title + one-liner is not enough product. Each book needs a real short description. His words: that is where we must be super good, because the blurb is what makes someone want the book.
  4. Instagram singles should be carousels. Hook (often a news item) → build the "I wish I understood what's going on" feeling → CTA to the book.
  5. On the three Hormuz cards: (a) why is one slide black and one blue — no reason visible; (b) slide 1 ("2,000 ships stuck, exit is 21 miles wide") is a fact, not a hook — it does not make him swipe.

What we decided

  • All five entered QUEUE.json as active: waitlist-simplify, covers-declutter, book-blurbs, carousel-format, news-hook-fix.
  • Conceded the ballot outright. The 2026-08-31 presale decision (shelf-as-ballot, picks travel with the email) is dead. We lose the per-title demand signal; Matt judged the reader-facing cost higher than the data. Do not re-propose it.
  • The Brevo blocker is also dead for now — he does not want an email provider yet. Simplest possible storage instead.
  • Colour on the news carousel was rhythm, not meaning. It gets a stated rule in design.md or it goes.

Open questions

  • Where does the email actually land with no provider? Static form endpoint vs a flat file in the repo. Decide inside waitlist-simplify.
  • Do the per-title checkboxes survive as an invisible signal, or vanish entirely? Leaning vanish — half a ballot is still a ballot.

Pointers

  • data/covers/, data/news/news.json, data/cards/cards.json are the sources; all three rebuild via their local build.py.
  • bin/publish.sh must run after any change.
  • Canonical docs made false by this session, to rewrite as the items are worked: plan.md (shelf-as-ballot section), design.md (cover rules, card colour rule), catalogue.md (needs blurbs), distribution.md (evergreen becomes a carousel).
