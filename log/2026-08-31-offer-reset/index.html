Offer reset — 2026-08-31

What we decided

  • $9 a book, $69 for the Essential Sixteen against a $144 shelf price. Bundle is the pushed offer and lives on our store only — Amazon has no bundle mechanic we can use.
  • No KDP Select. Its 90-day exclusivity forbids selling the ebook anywhere else including our own site (10% excerpt aside). That would delete the bundle, the free three chapters and the direct list. KENP page-reads do not buy that back.
  • Amazon's job is discovery and reviews, not margin. $9 nets ~$6.24 there vs ~$8.55 on Payhip. We keep it because it is the only cold-stranger source.
  • Paperback out for now. Per-title interior PDF + spine-dependent cover spread × 16, against 3–5 h/week, and print has a separate BSR so it does not lift the ebook. Revisit as one credibility object once a title is proven.
  • The bundle sells from book one as "the shelf as it fills" — true count printed by the price, new books emailed on ship day. Made honest by an unconditional refund until book sixteen ships, and a public shipping record.
  • Book one is Money, not The Dollar Empire. offer.md still said the old title.

What we tried and abandoned

  • Waiting for 8 books before selling the bundle — safe, wastes the launch.
  • Preorders, both Amazon (72-hour file deadline, one-year penalty) and direct prepay (EU withdrawal rights on digital goods).
  • An Amazon box-set ASIN now — allowed in principle, but nothing official confirms publishing one before the component books exist.

Open questions

  • Payhip vs Gumroad is assumed, not decided. Payhip's ~5% is the reference.
  • The refund window is generous by design. If abuse shows up, it is a policy we can tighten — but not before we have buyers.
