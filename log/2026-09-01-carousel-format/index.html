Carousels: the evergreen format

What we decided

Four slides, four named beats, one per book. THE HOLE (a belief stated so the reader feels the crack — it must not resolve on the slide), THE ANCHOR (one checkable institution, date or object), THE FORCE (the mechanism named in plain words), THE BOOK (what the book does, title in the callout, COMING SOON · LINK IN BIO in the bottom rail).

The beats are the house-style pendulum turned into slides, so the evergreen format and the news format are visibly the same mind at two speeds.

Colour got a job. Slides 1 to n-1 carry the book's cover colour (Tools and Stories ink, Communication and Power field_blue); the last slide flips to the other field, so the ask announces itself before a word is read. Written into design.md as the carousel colour rule — it covers the news carousels too, so the open news-hook-fix item now only owes a slide-1 rewrite and a re-render.

What we tried and abandoned

  • Rebuilding the ten old single cards one-for-one. Four were brand copy with no mechanism and no book to point at; six were all The Dollar Empire. So: the four brand cards stay singles (always ink), and the ten carousels are ten different books — the five in publishing order plus five across all four parts.
  • The book title set large on the last slide. It competes with the line that does the selling. Title moved into the callout rail instead.
  • Three slides (matching the news format): the ask would share a slide with the payoff, and the payoff is the screenshot-worthy part.
