News hook fix (2026-09-01)

Matt's two complaints, both fixed. news-hook-fix is done and the queue is empty.

What we decided

Slide 1 of a news carousel carries the hole, not the fact. The old slide 1 — "Two thousand ships are stuck in the Gulf. The exit is 21 miles wide" — is true, sourced, and finished. A finished sentence gives a scroller nothing to swipe for. The new one is:

Everyone says Hormuz. Nobody says why one country can move your fuel price.

The shape is "Everyone says X. Nobody says why Y." It names the word he has seen in headlines all week, names his own fuel price, and leaves the question open — and slide 2 (Britain leaves the Gulf in 1971, Iran takes the islands before the UAE exists) is the only thing that closes it. This is the hooks.md law applied to slide 1: the numbers moved to slide 2, where they answer instead of opening nothing. distribution.md beat 1 is renamed THE HOLE IN THE NEWS and bans a statistic on slide 1 outright.

Colour was already solved — the news cards just were not obeying it. The carousel rule written during carousel-format says a carousel is one post and so one colour: the book's cover colour for slides 1..n-1, and the last slide, the ask, flips to the other field. The Hormuz set was ink/blue/ink, which is alternation and means nothing. It is now ink/ink/blue, because The Barrel is Part I (Tools) and Part I is ink. design.md now says explicitly that news carousels obey the same rule — no second colour system for a second format.

What we did not do

  • Invent a separate colour rule for news cards. Considered and dropped: two rules for two formats is how a design system rots. The existing rule already answers the question, it was simply not applied.
  • Touch slides 2 and 3. They pass. The only defect was slide 1 and the fields.
