Blurb eval, run 1 — Surplus

What we decided

The six-question audit in hooks.md was never a gate. It passed the Surplus blurb and Matt rejected it. It checks legibility, not desire. It stays as a floor; the new eval decides.

The eval is at data/evals/surplus/eval.md. Three criteria taken verbatim from Matt's three complaints — I don't understand what it's about / I'm not hyped / I don't get how it helps me understand the current world — scored 0-3 by cold judges, plus one binary gate: would you pay $9 having read nothing else? Ships on 3/3 Yes with no criterion below 2.

The eval validated itself on run 1. The blurb that was live went in as an unlabelled control and was rejected by all three judges. It reproduces Matt's verdict, which is the only reason to trust it.

Shipped: the Turin strike blurb (K10). Deir el-Medina, year 29 of Ramesses III — the workmen cutting the pharaohs' tombs struck because the grain rations were late; the papyrus is in the Museo Egizio. Only unanimous Yes. Subject 3.0, hype 3.0, leverage 2.67. Live on /covers/ and in catalogue.md. Shipped verbatim — editing the winner after judging would void the run.

Three findings, and they apply to all sixteen books

Now written into hooks.md.

  1. Gesture at the category, never name the instance. 11 of 13 drafts failed this and all three judges found it independently. Naming "your rent" and then promising to explain your rent spends the promise inside the blurb.
  2. Show one checkable thing. Every Yes contained a verifiable object. Every No asserted a chain and asked to be believed.
  3. A scene beats a mechanism. The winner was the only draft with people in it.

Plus a warning: six of thirteen drafts sold a famous Sapiens chapter. Novelty must come from the evidence, not the thesis.

What we tried and abandoned

  • Strategy D attacked the premise — retitle to The Spare Mouth, The Granary or The Ratchet, and drop Surplus from position 01. All three reframes scored below the winner and The Spare Mouth was the draft one judge said he would be most embarrassed to own. The title and the launch position are not the problem — the blurb was. Do not re-open this without new evidence.
  • Considered hand-editing K10 to fix its one leverage-2 score. Rejected: changing the winner after the judging invalidates the run. Re-judge or ship as-is.
