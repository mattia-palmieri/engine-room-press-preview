Blurbs did not sell — the hook audit

What happened

Matt quoted the Surplus deck and blurb back and said: "I wouldn't read this book. I don't understand what it's about. I don't understand why it's of interest to me." Plus the root cause, in his words: the writer has all the context and forgets the reader has none. That message arrived at 17:53 and the next agent reply was about carousels — it went unanswered for a round. Check the tail for content, not just for whether an agent line comes after Matt's.

What we decided

docs/hooks.md is new and canonical — one law for blurbs, deck lines and carousel slide 1. A six-question cold-reader test (subject, whole sentence, today, picture, stakes, advance) plus a seventh that only appears in bulk.

The five failures in the Surplus blurb, all diagnosable: subject never named in plain words ("farming" appeared nowhere); riddle opening ("Grain keeps. That is the whole problem."); nothing from the reader's own week; a thesis where a picture belongs; and the blurb restating the deck.

What worked — the method, and reuse it

A writer agent and a cold-reader agent, separate contexts. The cold reader is told to read only the blurb JSON — no style guide, no positioning, no author intent. Its blindness is the whole value. Three rounds: 10/16 → (fixes) → 14/16 clean.

The finding neither the writer nor I could have made alone: every one of the sixteen closed with "This book is/follows/explains…", and Money/Dollar and Wire/Feed opened on the same image. Each blurb passed on its own; the page failed. Now capped at five of sixteen. That rule is in hooks.md.

What we abandoned

  • My own hand-patches of Dollar and Bomb — a live subagent was still editing the same file and overwrote them, and its versions were better. Do not edit a file a subagent still holds. Wait for the task notification, or snapshot.
  • The second cold read (coldread-v3.md) is worthless — it ran against a mid-flight file. Ignore it; coldread-final.md is the real one.

Open questions

  • Two known soft spots, left in on purpose rather than over-polished: Money's closer is the weakest of the sixteen, and Barrel/Chokepoint still sit close together as subjects.
  • The decks were only lightly touched. If Matt is still cold on a book, the deck is the next thing to attack, not the blurb.
  • The same audit has not yet been run on the carousel slide-1 hooks or the landing page. news-hook-fix is still active and hooks.md now governs it.
