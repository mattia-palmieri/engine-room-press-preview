The news-led format

What we decided

A ratio, not a switch: 2 news-led carousels a week, 3 evergreen. Full reasoning in DECISIONS.md. News is the door, the essentials are the house.

The format is three beats, always in this order: the news (one hard sourced fact from this week) → how we got here (one decision, decades back, named and dated) → the force (name it, name the book). Card 3's bottom rail carries the title and catalogue number.

One design difference from an evergreen card: the top rail carries the date in signal red instead of NO. 04. These posts are perishable; the card should say so. Everything else is the standard 1080×1350 template.

Three rules that keep us out of the commentary business (now in distribution.md): never say what should happen; every fact sourced before it is set, with the research file kept next to the cards; and only run the format when the news maps cleanly to one of the sixteen forces.

What we tried and abandoned

  • All-news. Perishable, drags toward opinion, and most news does not map to a catalogue force. Forcing the mapping makes cards that sell nothing.
  • Putting the book in card 3's kicker. The callout tag already reads 03 and "THE BARREL · BOOK 03" beside it read as a duplicate number. Moved the book to the bottom rail, which is where the CTA belongs anyway. build.py now takes an optional foot per card.

Open questions

  • The ratio is a guess. Four weeks of posting replaces it. If news carousels out-reach evergreen by a wide margin, go 3/2 or 4/1.
  • Who watches the news? Right now a subagent researches on demand. At two carousels a week that becomes a standing job — probably a weekly research round that produces a shortlist of stories already mapped to catalogue forces.
  • Captions. captions is still todo and now needs a news-carousel variant too.
