Book blurbs — the sixteen descriptions

Matt's note: "title + one liner is not enough… the small chapter describing the book is where we need to be super good." This is that job.

What we decided

One blurb shape, used sixteen times. Open on a tension the reader already half-feels, name the machinery in plain words, close on what changes in his head. 60–100 words. No dates, no proper nouns doing no work, no exclamation, no "discover" / "journey" / "delve" register. Written against house-style.md §4 (long-long-short rhythm, physical metaphor, "we" over "you").

The blurb lives in covers.json, not in the HTML and not only in the doc. covers.json is now the single source for all sixteen books: number, part, part name, colour field, title, deck, blurb. shelf.py renders the page from it and docs/catalogue.md was regenerated from the same records. One place to edit.

The shelf layout had to change to carry them. A 90-word paragraph under a 4-up thumbnail grid is unreadable. Now every book is a row: 120px cover on the left (96px under 400px wide), title + deck + blurb on the right. Two columns on desktop, one column under 900px. The four covers of a part still sit adjacent, so the series look survives.

Bug found and fixed

/covers/ had Part II labelled Stories and Part III labelled Communication — swapped. Cause: shelf.py kept its own hand-written PARTS list beside the partname field already in covers.json, and the two drifted. The page shipped wrong for about a day. Fix: PARTS is now derived from covers.json, so a second list cannot drift again. Comment left in the file saying why.

What we did not do

  • No per-book landing pages. The blurb is the product page for now. Sixteen routes for sixteen unwritten books is scaffolding for a need that has not arrived.
  • No sample chapter or "look inside". Nothing is written yet, so there is nothing honest to show.

The phone fix, made before the audit came back

The first layout put the blurb in the right-hand column beside the cover at every width. On a 390px phone that column is about 198px wide, roughly 26 characters a line — a 90-word paragraph turns into a 35-line ribbon. Caught by arithmetic, not by a screenshot. The row is now a CSS grid: under 560px the blurb drops below the cover and takes the full width. Cover shrinks to 96px there.

The phone audit then ran against the fixed page and found no defects at a verified true 390x844 (96px thumbnail, every row 342px, no horizontal overflow, blurb readable) or at 1280x900. Part labels confirmed correct in order, and the email form's error and thank-you states both work. data/audits/covers-blurbs-phone.md.
