What we decided

The Harari line — the biggest question he asked. Method yes, his words no. We write in his method freely (structure and ideas are not protectable in the US or EU). We never put a verbatim Sapiens quote on an Instagram card: a repeat commercial campaign on his sentences is the weakest fair-use pattern in the US and falls outside the EU/Italian quotation right, which exists for criticism, not marketing. Where his argument is the hook, we paraphrase it, name him, and answer it — legally free and a better card, because disagreement hooks harder than agreement. "For readers who loved Sapiens" is fine in captions and descriptions, and banned from every KDP metadata field (explicit Amazon rule, enforced). Full research: docs/notes/harari-ip.md.

Price — Matt's numbers, not ours: $9 a book, $69 bundle marked from $149, bundle as the hero. Written into offer.md. The consequence he may not have seen: Amazon has no bundle mechanic, so the offer we push can only exist on our own store. That reframes Amazon from "the shop" to "the discovery and reviews machine". Working it out properly is offer-reset.

Docs refresh (first ever run). Phase named at the top of index.md: pre-launch, mid-course-correction. brief.md demoted to notes/founding-brief.md — every one of its "open questions" is now answered elsewhere, so it was actively misleading a fresh reader. working-mode.md rewritten: the "away period, 3 days" framing was false (Nightshift is the standing mode), the Artifact instruction was dead (no Artifact tool exists in this session), and seven operational traps that had only ever lived in session files got promoted into it.
