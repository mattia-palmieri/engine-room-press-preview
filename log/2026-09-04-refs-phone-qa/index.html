Refs picker: phone QA

Short round. Nothing was unanswered in Telegram, and the one active item (references-picks) is blocked on Matt pasting his cover picks. Rather than nag him a third time — I had messaged 5 minutes earlier — I checked the thing we were asking him to use.

Why this round existed

Two rounds died waiting on picks because the picker only lived on localhost:8723, which a phone cannot open. That mistake has one root cause: we never tested the artefact against reality. Publishing it to /refs/ fixed the address. It did not prove the page works. This round proves it.

What we decided

Verify before asking again. If we are going to spend Matt's attention on one action, the action must be known-good first. Repeating an ask is cheap for us and expensive for him.

What we found — all pass

Live URL, real GitHub Pages, headless Chrome at 390x844 with a mobile UA:

  • Page loads; COVERS is the first section, no hunting.
  • All 16 cover images render (lazy-load, then naturalWidth > 0).
  • Like / No change state visibly on tap.
  • COPY MY PICKS works: clipboard write succeeds, a "Copied" modal with a Done button confirms it, and the fallback textarea shows the exact text so he can grab it by hand if the clipboard is blocked.
  • No horizontal overflow: scrollWidth 390 = viewport 390.
  • No console errors.
  • Assets checked with plain curl too: /refs/, cover jpgs and site pngs all 200 with real byte counts.

One nit, now fixed: the Like / No buttons were 38px tall on a phone, under the 44px thumb minimum. They are min-height:46px, font 15px, and republished.

Full write-up and three screenshots: data/refs-qa/PHONE-TEST.md. Kept out of data/refs/ on purpose — that directory is published verbatim, and QA notes are not for Matt.
