Weekend plan — 2026-08-27

Matt leaves tomorrow for ~3 days, phone only. This session existed to agree what the agent works on while he is away, and to fill an empty QUEUE.json.

What we decided

  • Goal: the first EUR 1 from a paying stranger. Matt explicitly rejected modelling a monthly salary. See DECISIONS.md.
  • Order: business → distribution → brand/landing → book. Matt's framing: "whether we can write nice books is not a real question." The real questions are competitors alive and dead, whether the interest is real, whether it is saturated, and what the angle is.
  • Never conclude "not viable." Matt believes a viable angle exists and that a flat no means we were too strict. The bar is low — sustain one person.
  • Books stay the core. A progress-tracking support platform or PWA and a newsletter are allowed. Video and podcasting are out — too complex to sustain.
  • Artifact rule suspended for the away period. On Telegram, an Artifact link is the only way Matt can see a page or a long document.
  • Telegram cadence: adaptive. Message on blockers and on milestones. No scheduled status reports.

What we tried and abandoned

  • Proposing a 3-day build (landing page + content package + sample chapter). Matt cut it: too much building before the business question is answered.
  • Asking Matt to choose a monthly income target. He refused the frame — see above.
  • Planning around the Nightshift harness. It is not set up yet. Matt is still building it and said not to worry about enrolment. .env and chat.jsonl do not exist in this project; INBOX.md still stands in for chat.jsonl until they do.

Open questions

  • Nothing blocking. Every queue task is self-contained by design.
  • The angle, the channel, the price and the format are all open by intention — they are what the queue exists to settle.

Pointers

  • QUEUE.json — 13 tasks, phased: 4 market research → angle → 3 distribution → offer → 30-day plan → brand → landing → stretch book outline.
  • docs/working-mode.md — new canonical doc: the away-period operating rules.
  • Nightshift harness: ~/Documents/nightshift, docs/design.md. Its new-project.md enrolment recipe is partly stale.
  • Research must go to Sonnet subagents writing straight to files in docs/notes/, never reported back into the main context.
