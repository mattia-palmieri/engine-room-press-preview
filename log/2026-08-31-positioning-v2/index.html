2026-08-31 — Positioning v2

What we decided

The reader is not "someone who bounced off history." He may have done well at school and still cannot explain why the dollar is the world's money. The gap is mechanism, not knowledge. That is what Harari actually delivers: tools let humans do new things, communication multiplies group size, shared stories let strangers cooperate, and whatever scales cooperation wins. Those four are our subject.

New one sentence: "School taught you what happened. These books explain how it works — one force at a time, and you finish each one in an evening."

Added promise 3, mechanism not events, with a chapter test: could the reader now explain this to someone else without looking anything up?

docs/positioning.md rewritten whole (not patched). Decision logged with the rejected options.

Two consequences the next session must carry

  1. Hard filter on catalogue. A title must name a force. If it cannot finish "this is how X let humans do something they could not do before," it is not our book. Rules out period books and event books on sight.
  2. The curriculum angle moved. Mechanism as the subject makes the catalogue one explanation in parts — tools, communication, stories, power — rather than a shelf of topics. Still not the opening sell, but it is now the natural shape, so catalogue should be ordered, not just listed.

What we tried and abandoned

  • Keeping "bounced off history" — smaller audience, and it promises history-but-nicer.
  • Leading on "no dates to memorise" — says what we removed, not what he gets. Demoted to promise 2.

Open questions
