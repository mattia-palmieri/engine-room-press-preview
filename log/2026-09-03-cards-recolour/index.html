Cards recoloured to match the covers

What we decided

Colour on a card is derived, never stored. data/cards/cards.json used to carry a field: ink|blue key per carousel and a part key. Two sources of truth for the same fact, and they had already drifted — the covers went to four colours on 2026-09-03 and the cards stayed on two. The field key is deleted. build.py now reads part and looks it up in a FIELD map identical to the one in data/covers/build.py. A card and its cover cannot disagree again without someone editing both files.

Tools = iron black #14171A, Communication = steel blue #173B4E, Stories = oxidised copper #17342A, Power = aubergine #251830.

The ask slide flips to steel blue, or to iron black when the book is already a Communication book. This was already written in design.md; it just was not built.

Forty-four cards re-rendered and published. The three news cards (/news/) already complied — ink, ink, blue — so they were not touched.

What we did NOT do

No material hatch on the cards. The covers carry the ISO section hatch; the cards carry the full grid. That split is design.md rule 7 and it is deliberate — the card is the shouted surface and the hatch competes with the headline at feed size. Matt's "too subtle, too boilerplate" note was about covers, and it was answered on the covers. Resisted the urge to spray hatches everywhere for consistency's sake.

Why this round, and not the eval

The one active item, blurb-eval-surplus, needs Matt to name one blurb he would buy. He was asked last round and has not answered; nothing an agent does can meet that done_when. Rather than idle, this round finished a job the docs themselves recorded as unfinished — design.md carried a written admission that the built cards were wrong. That is committed work, not new work.
