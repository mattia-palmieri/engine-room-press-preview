Source access — 2026-08-27

Before three days of unattended research, we checked what the agent can actually read. Better to find a blocked source with Matt at the desk than mid-run.

What we decided

  • Test sources before trusting a research plan. Nine sources smoke-tested. Full results and the read commands live in docs/notes/source-access.md.
  • Amazon is solved and needs no ritual. The bot wall is triggered by a browser profile with no Amazon cookies, not by deep links or a cold daemon. The profile at ~/.agent-browser/profiles/real-browser now holds those cookies and they persist across rb close and reboot. Deep links work.
  • BSR is per edition. Sapiens paperback was #62,645 in Books; the Audible edition #515 in Audible. Same book, two orders of magnitude. Never compare rank across formats — this would have produced badly wrong demand conclusions.
  • The Amazon URL slug is cosmetic. Only the node id is real: /Best-Sellers-Books-World-History/zgbs/books/4999/ returns Iran History. Confirm against the page title. books/9 = History.
  • Git initialised. Three days of unattended doc-rewriting with no undo was a real risk. chat.jsonl is gitignored — the harness owns it, it is a transcript.

What we tried and abandoned

  • "Keep the Chrome session alive." Written into the docs as fact after a single success, then challenged by Matt and disproved by test — closing is safe. This was speculation stated as certainty. The correction is in DECISIONS.md as an explicit correction, so the stale claim cannot be read and believed.
  • Delegating the Amazon test to a subagent. Matt could not see the browser moving and wanted it driven directly. For anything Matt is watching live, or any short interactive browser debug, drive it yourself — delegation hides the work.
  • Signing into Amazon in the agent profile. Prepared as the fallback, never needed. No login is required.
  • WebFetch for archive.org. Fully blocked; rb works.

Open questions

  • Google Trends comparison charts are JS-flaky — the shell loads, the data call fails intermittently. Retry with a longer wait, or query one term at a time.
  • Amazon cookies may expire eventually. If a bot wall ever reappears, the re-seed recipe is in docs/notes/source-access.md.
