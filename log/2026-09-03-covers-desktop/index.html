Two separate problems, so two separate choices, on one page: https://mattia-palmieri.github.io/engine-room-press-preview/covers/options/

Diagnosis of the live page (why he is right)

Screenshotted at 1440px. Four faults, all measurable:

  1. The covers are 120px wide on a 1440px screen. The best asset we own is a postage stamp and the page is ~70% grey text.
  2. A dead gap under every cover. The blurb sits in grid-row:2 of the item grid, so it starts below the cover and below the deck — leaving an empty block to the left of the blurb, ~150px tall, sixteen times. That is the white space he saw.
  3. The page is capped at 1080px. On a 1440 screen a third of the desktop is empty margin and the grid lines drift off the text.
  4. No hierarchy. Deck and blurb are both Newsreader at 17/16px. Nothing tells the eye where to start.

What was built

Decision 1 — three layouts, all sixteen books, real blurbs, all working.

  • A · Shelf — covers big, four across, part rows. Title + deck only; the blurb is behind a <details> click. The page sells the covers. Cost: a click to read.
  • B · Ledger — one full-width row per book: number, cover chip, title+deck, blurb. Nothing hidden, nothing ragged, no dead space. Cost: covers stay small.
  • C · Spread — one band per book, 220px cover on the left (sticky while you read), the whole entry beside it. Cost: the longest page.

Decision 2 — seven cover textures, same title, same type, shown big and at 160px (Amazon) and 96px (feed), on both ink and field_blue: control (today), drafting sheet (fine mesh + heavy line every 250px), dimension lines (the title measured, drafting-style, with corner ticks), section hatch (45° hairlines), halftone screen, paper grain (SVG noise, no pattern), split field (one hairline, lighter tint above).

Decisions taken on Matt's behalf

  • Show, do not describe. He asked for "options"; options he has to imagine are worthless. Everything is a real page with real content.
  • A texture, never an illustration. design.md says the title is the cover and sixteen must read as one imprint. Every treatment is a surface, not art, so it survives the thumbnail test — which is why each is shown at 96px too.
  • No new colours. All seven textures are paper-white at low opacity on the two existing fields.
  • Signal red stayed rationed (design.md rule 3): the first drafts had a red "+" on all sixteen items in A and a red number tag on all sixteen in C. Both pulled back to ink.

Traps hit and paid for

  • Inline SVG noise needs literal width/height attributes. position:absolute; inset:0 does not size an <svg> — it rendered at its default 300×150 in the top corner and the render still looked plausible. Also mix-blend-mode:overlay cancels itself on a near-black field; plain low opacity is what works.
  • Halftone and grain at "tasteful" opacity are invisible. First pass at .16/.05 looked identical to the control. Textures on a dark field need roughly double what looks right in the CSS.
