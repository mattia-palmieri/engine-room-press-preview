Engine Room Press — evergreen carousels

Ten four-slide carousels, one per book, 1080×1350. Every carousel runs the same four beats: the hole in something you believe, the anchor that broke it, the force underneath, then the book.

Colour is not decoration. Slides 1–3 carry the book’s part colour, the same colour as its cover. The last slide flips to the other colour, so the ask announces itself before a word is read.
