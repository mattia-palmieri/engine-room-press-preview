Engine Room Press — why this is happening

The news-led format, proved on the Strait of Hormuz. A three-card carousel that starts from a story the reader already saw this week, walks back to the decision that built it, and lands on the book that explains the force.

  1. The news. One hard fact from this week. No opinion.
  2. How we got here. One decision, decades back, that set it up.
  3. The force. Name it, and name the book.

The date in the top rail, in red, replaces the card number. These cards are perishable and they should say so.
